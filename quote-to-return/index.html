Optional AI assistance

Ask for a change, check the preview, then decide.

1

Describe what is different

Ask for different pricing, fields, booking buffers, documents, branding, or a supported integration.

2

Check the proposed changes

See what would change, what is not supported, and how much of your customization allowance it would use.

3

Approve or discard

Try double-booking and pricing examples first. Nothing changes until you approve it, and earlier versions remain available.

AI can still get things wrong.

It may misunderstand an instruction, miss an unusual case, import a field incorrectly, or write text that needs editing. Some requests will not be supported. The assistant cannot change payment processing or publish changes on its own. You remain responsible for checking prices, availability, taxes, contracts, and safety requirements.
