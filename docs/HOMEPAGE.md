# howork products homepage contract

The homepage is the public portfolio for products operated by Huikang Wang. It
is not a blog index or a claim that every listed product is available for sale.

## Product inventory

The homepage must summarize these products and preserve their current public
status:

| Product | Public summary | Public status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 老纳 (Shelf Life) | iOS shelf-life, storage, iCloud sync, and expiry reminders for household items | Public support and privacy pages are available |
| QuoteToReturn | Planned hosted rental workflow for small event-rental teams, with an immediately usable baseline and bounded AI-assisted customization | Validation prototype; not available for live purchase |
| 花哪 | Local-first iPhone and Android bookkeeping from natural language or on-device speech, with local statistics and Excel export | Android released; iPhone App Store submission in progress, with real version-matched destinations |

QuoteToReturn may show its planned USD 39/month price only when the same text
also says that live purchase is unavailable. Future products must not be shown
as released, purchasable, or hosted until that is true.

## Shared positioning

The homepage uses ordinary customer language. It describes what each product
helps someone do, its main functions, any setup requirement the customer must
know, and whether it can currently be used or purchased. Architecture terms,
internal validation language, and product-development philosophy do not belong
in public product copy.

The hero states the brand's customer value. It must not enumerate the catalog,
narrate what appears below, explain the page structure, or restate the status
labels already shown on product cards.

## Public navigation

The homepage must link to the existing Shelf Life support/privacy pages and the
site-wide Terms and Privacy Policy. Products without a public release page must
not receive a fake or placeholder purchase link. 花哪 links to `/huana/`; its
App Store and Android destinations must remain real and version-matched.
